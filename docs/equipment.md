# Approved equipment catalogue

Implemented November 17, 2026. 74 active items: 32 weapons, 22 body armors, 10 shields, 10 Head items. Existing accessories and all other old gear remain owned in a legacy vault and are excluded from new stock and loot. Accessory designs were approved later the same day (see Accessories below) and are not built yet.

## Rules

Slots: Main hand, Off hand, Armor, Head, two Accessories. Two-handed weapons occupy both hands. A shield or second one-handed weapon uses the Off hand; an off-hand basic strike uses 50% strength. This dual-wield value is an initial balance choice, not a settled final number. A two-handed item automatically returns the off-hand item to the bag. Identical clothing categories (boots, gloves, cloaks) cannot be doubled in the two accessory slots; different rings can. Every class can equip any item. No preferred-weapon damage multiplier. Equipment cannot change during a current or saved battle.

Starter kits are issued once per class, cannot be sold, and do not duplicate on reload. Switching to a newly issued class equips its kit and returns previous hand/body items to the bag. Fine equivalents appear in ordinary shops and dungeon chests. Legendary rewards come from bosses; deeper equipment comes from later floors. Specialty stock is separate from the ordinary shop and themed to draining regions.

Resistance sources use the strongest value, not addition: two sources of 50% Acid resistance still mean 50%, never immunity. Resistance affects direct elemental damage; Poison and Bleed have separate immunity rules. Damage-type boosts add together. Head percentage bonuses apply after flat equipment stats.

A shield's damage-to-ATP conversion is calculated after Armor/resistance, Block and Rage, before Basophil sharing. The converted portion is prevented even at full ATP. Bite Back uses damage prevented specifically by Block, then applies the attacker's Physical Armor. Phagocytizing Shield heals for actual HP removed, excluding overkill; the next damaging action consumes its opportunity.

Enchanted immunity never changes the lore into a medical claim. Staff of Carbs is intentionally a breadstick; Old Moldy Stick's Poison is a fantasy mechanic, not penicillin's real action.

## The Golgi Workshop

A Fibroblast enchanter is available in every lymph node town (✧ on the map and a shop-panel shortcut). Add Acid, Electrical, Oxidative or Toxin resistance to owned modern body armor. One added enchantment per individual copy; replacing it costs the full price. Built-in properties remain. No immunity enchantments. Initial cost: 250 + 10 × player level glucose. Enchantment metadata travels with that copy through equipping, selling, saves and backups.

## ATP and Wizard rules

ATP is spent when a usable ability question appears, before the answer. Correct succeeds; wrong fails and spends the turn with no refund. Target selection can be cancelled before the question, without cost. Basic and enemy attacks have no new random miss rule. Reloading a question does not charge ATP again.

Wizard gets two spells from the main-hand staff and always retains Membrane Barrier. Without a compatible staff, use the basic Pepsin pair. Staff attack-power bonuses affect basic Attack; they do not silently alter spell Power.

## Accessories (approved design, not yet built)

Approved November 17, 2026. 25 accessories replace the legacy accessory pool for new loot and shops; the 79 legacy accessories stay owned in the vault. Accessories avoid raw stat bloat and the Head slot's large multipliers. Each category has a job: boots move and explore, gloves change your attacks, cloaks resist statuses and keep you alive, rings are small stackable effects. Boots, gloves and cloaks cannot be doubled; two different rings or charms can. No sets, no class-locked accessories. Status-duration effects never reach immunity. Numbers are initial playtest values. ## Persistence and migration

Profile version 8 preserves existing gear IDs, classes, progression and private study data. Existing shield/head/glove pieces are moved to the appropriate slots, or safely returned to the bag if conflicting. Sets and old subclass upgrades remain stored but suspended. New active gear uses distinct IDs, avoiding silent conversion of existing rare items into starters. Old approved display-name changes are retained for legacy owners.

Coat of Mucous retains 25% of escrowed glucose on Flee/Leave, not XP or found items. Marrow Helm retains 25% escrowed XP on death, not glucose or found items. Lymph Drain and clearing bank the full expedition as before.

## Validation

Run `node tests/equipment.cjs`, `node tests/combat-rework.cjs`, and `node tests/content-unlocks.cjs`. These cover the catalogue, ownership and migration, hand constraints, per-copy enchanting, spell kits, wrong-answer costs, shields, armor/head/weapon effects, reward retention, and DOM menu rendering. Browser visual QA is not included.
